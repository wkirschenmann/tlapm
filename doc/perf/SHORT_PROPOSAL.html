tlapm · performance

Optimize performance on large proof files

On a 9 967-obligation refinement chain — preparation: OOM >12 GB after 39 min → 357 MB and 55.0 s.

branch tlapm-perf-short21 commits, 20 files, +1202 / −282base 4600b24

Machine assistance has made it realistic to generate TLA+ proofs rather than write them by hand. What comes out is markedly more verbose than what a person writes, and much longer: specifications carrying ten or twenty thousand obligations are no longer unusual.

tlapm was proven on proofs of a different size. It is comfortable on small ones and does not scale to these: the cost of an obligation grows with the file it sits in, so the work grows faster than the proof does.

We went looking for where that growth comes from. It is localised — a handful of places where a non-linear cost sits inside a loop over the context — which is why what follows is twelve pull requests against the existing design.

02

One mechanism, four consequences

tlapm generates one obligation per proof node, and each obligation carries its whole context: the module scope, every definition in scope, and the statement of every theorem proved before it. The context therefore grows with the file, and the per-obligation work is a function of the context. Generating all of them is therefore O(n²).

That single fact has four distinct consequences, and this series has one family of change for each:

Repeated passes over the context

Expanding k definitions walked the context k times; detecting ENABLED axioms compared every axiom against every hypothesis; every hypothesis lookup walked a deque.

PR2, PR3, PR7

Contexts kept alive

Every obligation was materialised before the first prover started, and a memo table pinned one context per obligation. Peak memory grew with the file.

PR4

Context that no longer matters

After expansion, hidden definitions and the statements of earlier theorems are unreachable from the goal, and are most of the weight shipped to the prover.

PR5

Context recomputed from scratch

Consecutive obligations share almost all of their context — on the 30k monolith, 699 of 743 hypotheses are the physically same objects as in the previous one — and all three preparation passes recomputed the whole thing.
